# Reusable Earth globe

Source: current earth-circumference/js/renderer-textured.js and createGL from globe-core.js, extracted 2026-11-04. Fragment and vertex shader bodies are unchanged; renderer has a disposal guard for async image loading. Existing experiment entrypoints are not changed. Assets remain the four actual current experiment assets.

Import createEarthGlobe from index.js; pass a sized DOM host. Optional `assetBase`, `minZoom`, `maxZoom`, and `snapDegrees` (default 15) configure loading, zoom limits, and Ctrl-drag angle steps. API: `rotate(dx,dy)` in CSS pixels, `setZoom(value)`, `setProjection('orthographic'|'north-polar')`, `setPolarOuterLatitude(degrees)` (clamped to 0..89N), `setTextureStyle('handdrawn'|'original')`, `setBrushEnabled(boolean)`, `setLightingMode('sunlit'|'diffuse'|'unlit')`, `setSunHighlight(boolean)`, `state` (copy), `destroy()`. All rotation is around the sphere origin; no translation state exists. The default view uses orthographic sphere projection, as the original renderer does. `north-polar` uses an azimuthal equidistant projection: 90N at the center, a configurable northern latitude at the rim (60N by default), and longitude as the radial angle. The preview includes preset views for 60N and the Tropic of Cancer at 23.44N. `original` switches both projections to `earth-eq-2048.png` and selects `brushEnabled=false` by default, while the brush can still be toggled manually. `sunlit` keeps the atmospheric warm highlight, `diffuse` removes the highlight while retaining directional lighting, and `unlit` displays the texture without directional shading.

Preview: ../../globe-preview.html. Projection, texture, lighting, brush, and polar-edge controls are independent, so every combination can be inspected. The polar-edge slider runs from 0N to 89N; changing it switches to the north-polar view. Drag or arrow keys rotate without polar clamps in 3D mode; horizontal drag and left/right keys rotate longitude in polar mode. Wheel, two-pointer pinch and +/- zoom. Hold Ctrl while dragging to lock the first dominant screen axis and snap to 15-degree steps. The component emits `earth-globe-constraint` with `{active, axis, angle, step}` during constrained gestures, `earth-globe-projection` with `{projection}` after projection changes, `earth-globe-texture` with `{textureStyle}` after texture changes, `earth-globe-lighting` with `{mode}` after lighting changes, `earth-globe-brush` with `{enabled}` after brush changes, and `earth-globe-polar-edge` with `{latitude}` after edge changes. ResizeObserver keeps the canvas centered; resize and interaction schedule a single draw. No continuous idle rendering. destroy aborts listeners, disconnects observer, cancels frames, guards pending image loads and loses its WebGL context.
